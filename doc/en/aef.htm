Wrapping the Board Inside MKPortal - AEF

STEP 1: Set Config Option

In MKPortal "Control Panel > Preferences > Forum Page Look & Feel" set the "Show Forum inside MKPortal?" option to "Yes".

STEP 2: Modify Board File(s)

Thanks to the generosity of the AEF developers, the AEF board comes pre-modified with MKPortal. There are no board files that must be edited by the end-user.
